The rules are enforced properly — team farthest from the cochonnet replays, cochonnet must land 6-10m from the circle, dead balls are dead. Three balls per player, first to 13.

The three throws are there: Demi-portée (rolling lob), Plombée (high-arc drop, tricky), Tir au Fer (direct strike, surgical when it works). Spin affects the trajectory on impact.

## ITCH.IO — Devlog #1

**Titre :** My grandfather played petanque every Sunday for 40 years. I made a game about it.

**Corps :**
```
When I was young, I spent summers in the south of France watching old men play petanque in the village square.

They took it completely seriously. The slow deliberation before each throw. The arguments about whose ball was closest — centimeters, sometimes millimeters. The sound when one metal ball hits another cleanly — a sharp, satisfying *clac* that echoes across the square.

I never found a video game that captured that feeling. So eventually I stopped waiting and built one.

---

Petanque Master is a free browser game — provençal pixel art, 12 characters, 5 terrains, and a progression system where your Rookie grows into a real player.

But the characters are what I care about most.

**Papi René** is the calm veteran. He doesn't intimidate. He doesn't trash-talk. He just plays consistently, match after match, and somehow that's more threatening than anything else. Beating him for the first time feels earned.

**Mamie Josette** is a different kind of threat. She talks. She moves. And in game terms, her special ability makes your aim cursor shake. It's completely unfair. It's also completely accurate to how it feels to play against someone who knows exactly how to get in your head.

**La Choupe** works the docks. He plays rough and wins a lot and talks the whole time. You know someone like him.

There are 9 more opponents. Each one taught me something about what makes petanque interesting.

---

The terrains were the other obsession. Village dirt is your introduction — predictable, forgiving, learn the basics. Sand is the opposite: the ball decelerates so hard that your whole sense of power goes wrong. You throw what felt like enough and it stops a meter short. The hill makes you constantly doubt your aim. Tiles on the Docks will surprise you with what bounces where.

I wanted each terrain to feel genuinely different without reading a description of why.

---

The game is live now. Free, no install — open the page and play.

**[LINK]**

If you've played real petanque and something feels wrong, I want to know. The physics were the part I cared most about getting right, and real players will notice things I missed.
```
